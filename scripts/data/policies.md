# DiagnoFlow Company Policy

## What DiagnoFlow Offers

DiagnoFlow is a diagnostic centre with an attached pharmacy in Bangladesh. The company offers diagnostic tests from its test catalogue, sample collection at the centre, laboratory processing, and one test report for each test in an order.

The company also offers pharmacy sales at the counter, including matching prescriptions to medicines, dispensing medicines, and advice from the pharmacist at the counter. Patients are registered at the front desk, and each patient gets a patient account and a patient number for their orders and reports.

All prices are in BDT. The company offers information through its chatbot about these policies, the test catalogue, and the pharmacy. It does not offer any service that is not listed in this policy or in the test catalogue.

## What DiagnoFlow Does Not Offer

DiagnoFlow does not offer doctor consultations, diagnosis, treatment, or prescription writing. A prescriber outside the centre decides what is prescribed. The centre only records the prescriber's name on a prescription.

DiagnoFlow does not offer emergency care, hospital admission, surgery, ambulance services, home sample collection, or home delivery of medicines. Tests and medicines are provided at the centre only. There is no online ordering or delivery service.

DiagnoFlow does not offer medical advice about a test result, a medicine, or a treatment decision. Those decisions belong to the patient's doctor. The company does not offer a guarantee of a test result, a diagnosis, or a cure.

## Diagnostic Tests: What the Company Bears

The price of a test bears the cost of sample collection at the centre, laboratory processing, consumables, and one report for that test. No extra laboratory charge is added beyond the price recorded on the order.

The company bears the cost of re-collection and a repeat test, free of charge, if the sample was insufficient or unsuitable because of laboratory handling, or if the test result was wrong because of a laboratory error. This applies only if the problem is reported within 7 days of the report being released and the laboratory confirms the error.

The company bears the cost of correcting an order if staff recorded the wrong patient details or the wrong test. The price recorded on an order is the price at the time the order was made, and the company bears any later catalogue price increase for that order.

## Diagnostic Tests: What the Company Does Not Bear

The company does not bear the cost of a repeat test if the patient did not follow the preparation instructions given at booking, arrived too late or missed the sample collection, gave wrong name, age, or phone details, or asks for a repeat test for reassurance or a second opinion.

The company does not bear the cost of an additional test that was not ordered, a doctor's fee, treatment costs, travel costs, lost income, or any other loss that follows from a test, a delay, or a result.

The company does not bear losses caused by delays outside its control, such as power failure, supplier failure, or a public holiday, or by a patient being unreachable on the phone number given. Staff will inform the patient of a delay, but the company does not pay for the consequences of the delay. If an order is cancelled after the sample has been collected, the company does not refund the price of that test.

## Test Reports: What the Company Bears

The company bears the cost of preparing one report for each test in an order, and of releasing it once the laboratory technician has prepared it. Only a laboratory technician prepares and releases a report.

If a report contains an error caused by the laboratory or by staff, the company bears the cost of a corrected report, and of a repeat test if a repeat test is needed to correct the result. The corrected report is provided free of charge if the error is reported within 7 days of the report being released.

The company bears responsibility for keeping reports confidential, and for showing a released report only to the patient it belongs to, or to staff who need it for their job.

## Test Reports: What the Company Does Not Bear

The company does not bear responsibility for interpreting a report, diagnosing a condition, or deciding treatment. That is the doctor's job, not the laboratory's. The company does not bear the cost of a doctor's visit, follow-up tests ordered by a doctor, or treatment that follows from a result.

The company does not bear responsibility if a patient loses a report, shares a report with another person, delays collecting a report, or acts, or fails to act, on a result without a doctor's advice.

The company does not bear responsibility for a report based on wrong information given by the patient, a sample taken elsewhere, or a result from another laboratory. If the patient gave the wrong details, the cost of correcting the record or repeating the test is not borne by the company.

## Pharmacy: What the Company Bears

The company bears responsibility for dispensing the correct medicine, with the correct brand or generic name, strength, form, and quantity, from a batch that is in date and has been stored correctly before sale. The company bears the cost of stock checks and expiry checks before a medicine is sold.

If the pharmacy dispenses the wrong medicine, the wrong strength, the wrong quantity, or an expired or damaged medicine, the company bears the cost of replacing it or refunding the amount paid for it. This applies only if the problem is reported at the counter, or within 24 hours with the receipt and the original packaging, and the pharmacy confirms the error.

The company bears the cost of advice given by the pharmacist at the counter about how to take and store a medicine, as directed by the prescriber.

## Pharmacy: What the Company Does Not Bear

The company does not bear the cost of treatment for a side effect, an allergic reaction, or an interaction with another medicine, if the medicine was dispensed correctly and taken as directed, and the patient did not tell the prescriber or the pharmacist about the allergy or the other medicine.

The company does not bear responsibility if a medicine is taken wrongly, shared with another person, taken after its expiry date, stored wrongly after sale, or taken without a prescription when a prescription was needed. Once medicine has left the pharmacy counter, it cannot be returned, except where the pharmacy itself made the error described above.

The company does not bear responsibility for a medicine bought elsewhere, a forged prescription, or a prescription with wrong information given by the patient. The company does not bear the cost of a medicine the patient no longer wants after it has been dispensed.

## Payments, Cancellations and Refunds: What the Company Bears

The company bears responsibility for giving a receipt showing the tests or medicines supplied and the amount paid, and for charging the price recorded on the order or at the counter, with no extra charge beyond what staff explain at the time.

If an order is cancelled before the sample has been collected, the company bears a full refund of the amount paid for that order, to the original payment method, within 7 working days. If the pharmacy made an error as described above, the company bears the replacement or refund of the amount paid for the affected medicine.

The company bears the cost of processing a refund it has agreed to, and of informing the patient if a refund is delayed.

## Payments, Cancellations and Refunds: What the Company Does Not Bear

The company does not bear bank charges, mobile money charges, or charges for a failed payment caused by the patient's bank or wallet. It does not bear a shortage of cash or change after the patient has left the counter, or a payment made to the wrong person or account by the patient.

The company does not refund the price of a test after the sample has been collected, a completed test, or a medicine that has been dispensed, except for a pharmacy error as described above. It does not refund an order cancelled because the patient changed their mind after sample collection or after processing started.

The company does not bear any loss beyond the amount paid for the affected test or medicine. It does not pay for travel, lost income, doctor's fees, treatment costs, or any other consequential loss.

## Patient Responsibilities

The patient is responsible for giving correct name, age, sex, and phone details, for following the preparation instructions given at booking, and for arriving on time for sample collection.

The patient is responsible for bringing the prescription, the patient number, and the receipt when asking about an order, a report, or a medicine, for checking the medicine, quantity, and change at the counter before leaving, and for taking and storing medicine exactly as the prescriber and pharmacist direct.

The patient is responsible for keeping passwords and one-time codes private, for not sharing an account or a report with another person, and for reporting an error promptly, within the time limits in this policy. If a patient fails in these responsibilities, the resulting cost or loss is not borne by the company, as set out in the sections above.

## Privacy and Data Protection

The company bears responsibility for keeping patient records confidential, for limiting staff access to the information needed for their own job, and for keeping the chatbot to summaries only, with no patient's or any other user's personal information shown to staff.

The company bears responsibility for correcting a record if the company recorded it wrongly, and for not selling patient data or sharing it except where needed for care, payment, or a legal requirement.

The company does not bear responsibility if a patient shares a password, a one-time code, an account, or a report with another person, posts a report publicly, or gives sensitive details in chat. Once information has been shared by the patient, the company cannot control how another person uses it.

## Complaints, Corrections and Claims

A complaint or claim should be made at the front desk, the laboratory, or the pharmacy, with the receipt, the patient number, and the order or report details. Report problems are accepted within 7 days of the report being released. Wrong or damaged medicine problems are accepted at the counter, or within 24 hours with the receipt and the original packaging.

The company bears responsibility for investigating a complaint, correcting its own error, and informing the patient of the outcome. Where the company is at fault, its responsibility is limited to repeating the affected test, correcting the report, or replacing or refunding the affected medicine or test, up to the amount paid for that item.

The company does not bear claims made after the time limits above, claims without a receipt or proof, claims about matters listed as not borne by the company in this policy, or compensation beyond the affected item. The chatbot cannot accept or decide a complaint. A complaint must be made to staff at the centre.

## Changes to This Policy

The company may change this policy. A changed policy applies to new orders and new medicine sales from the date it takes effect. An existing order keeps the price recorded on it, and an existing sale keeps the terms that applied at the counter.

The current policy is posted at the centre and given on request. Patients should check the current version at booking, because the chatbot answers from the version currently ingested into its knowledge base, and an older answer may no longer apply after a change.
